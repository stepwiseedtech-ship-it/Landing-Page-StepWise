Your Data Matters

Privacy Policy

At Stepwise, the security and privacy of our educational community are our top priorities. Because we handle sensitive academic and emotional data, we are committed to the highest standards of digital protection.

Our Commitment

Key Privacy Principles

Strict Data Protection

All academic records, grades, and personal information of students and teachers are encrypted and securely stored.

Confidentiality in the Emotions Inbox

The data collected through the "Emotions Inbox" is strictly confidential. It is only accessible to authorized school counselors to provide timely psychological support.

Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do not—and will never—sell, rent, or share personal student or teacher data with third-party companies for marketing purposes.

Beta Phase Disclaimer

Stepwise is currently in its funding and development stage. This demo environment is used solely for demonstration and research purposes stemming from our thesis project at Colombian educational institutions.
